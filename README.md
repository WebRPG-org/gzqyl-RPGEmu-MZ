# Notes
MZEmu App is just an offline tool app which could let you play those games on your iOS devices, it does not supply any games.\
Though you could download thoese games from website with Safari browser, MZEmu does not care about how you get the game files, it just give you the ability to play those games.\
It just likes my another emulator called "ROM Emulator", which could let you play GBA games on your iPhone or iPad.\
And just likes my another emulator called "RPGEmu", which could let you play RPG Maker MV games on your iPhone or iPad.\
The difference is MZEmu will let you play RPG Maker MZ games.
It likes the app "RPGEmu", but they are two different apps, one is for MZ, another is for MV, using MZEmu app could not play MV games, in other words, using RPGEmu could not play MZ games.
